## 4) Risk-First Questions (Must Answer)

### Market / Cycle Risk

- What scenario would make this market underperform for the next 3–5 years?
- What’s the leading indicator you watch for demand weakening here?
- What’s the “anti-thesis” suburb/segment we should avoid and why?

### Deal Risk

- What’s the most likely hidden issue on a property like this (e.g., drainage, structural, easements, zoning constraints)?
- What due diligence steps catch 80% of problems early?
- What is your walk-away rule (price / yield / zoning / condition)?

### Execution Risk

- If timelines blow out 2–3 months, what’s our contingency plan?
- What trades/consultants are critical path, and what do they cost?
- What approvals commonly delay projects in this council area?

### Finance / Cashflow Risk

- What holding costs should we budget per month (rates, insurance, vacancy, interest, utilities)?
- What buffer do you consider non-negotiable?
- What’s the realistic “bad case” cashflow and vacancy assumption?

## 6) Strategy-Specific Question Blocks

### A) Buy & Hold (Yield + Growth)

- What yield is realistic net vs gross here?
- What tenant profile dominates demand? Any concentration risk?
- How do you underwrite rent growth (assumptions)?

### B) Renovation / Value-Add

- What reno scope gives best ROI here (kitchen/bath/paint/floorplan)?
- What is the typical uplift range and how do you validate it?
- What’s the “renovation trap” we should avoid?

### C) Subdivision / Development

- What zoning and overlays matter most for feasibility?
- What are typical application timelines and success rates?
- What’s the minimum margin (or profit buffer) you require before proceeding?

### D) Dual Occ / Granny Flat

- What approvals are needed? Any council-specific gotchas?
- How does it affect valuer sentiment and resale liquidity?
- What is realistic rent for each dwelling and vacancy assumptions?

### E) Rooming / Co-Living

- What is the compliance checklist (fire, egress, parking, licensing)?
- Property management complexity: how is it handled and what fees?
- What’s the realistic occupancy profile and churn rate?

### F) NDIS/SDA / Specialist Accommodation (if applicable)

- What demand evidence do you use (participant numbers, provider pipeline, waitlists)?
- What is the vacancy risk and typical fill time?
- What are the design spec requirements (accessibility, layout, OOA/IL, safety)?
- What contracts govern income (who pays, terms, termination rights)?
- What is the reputational/ethical risk and how do you mitigate it?
