# Partnership Structures — QLD Side-by-Side Comparison *(M-101)*

> Identifier: **M-101** — Structure choice for first QLD resi SPV (Unit Trust with corporate trustee vs `Pty Ltd` company).
>
> General information only — not legal, tax, or financial advice. Obtain Queensland-specific professional advice before committing.

## Options Compared

- Unit Trust with Corporate Trustee
- Proprietary Limited Company (`Pty Ltd`)

> Scope: This note compares entity-based SPV options for the first QLD resi deal (Unit Trust vs `Pty Ltd`). Simple co-ownership / partnership (for example, tenants in common) is outlined at a higher level in `docs/03-partnership-kickoff-qld.md` under **Structure Options**.

## Summary — First QLD Resi SPV (2–4 Partners)

- Unit Trust: Common for AU property SPVs where a small group of investors want pass-through economics and flexible distributions. A corporate trustee (often a `Pty Ltd`) holds legal title; partners hold units in the trust.
- `Pty Ltd` Company: Simple, familiar structure where the company owns the property and the 2–4 partners hold shares. Profits are taxed at company level and distributed as dividends.

## Side-by-Side

### Legal Form & Ownership

- Unit Trust
  - Legal title held by corporate trustee; beneficial ownership via units.
  - Unitholder register; trust deed governs rights.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Company holds legal title; shareholders own shares.
  - Constitution + shareholders' agreement govern rights.

### Liability & Governance

- Unit Trust
  - Limited liability via corporate trustee; trustee owes fiduciary duties.
  - Governance via trust deed + trustee resolutions; unitholder meeting provisions.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Limited liability; directors' duties under Corporations Act.
  - Governance via board/constitution; shareholder resolutions.

### Tax Treatment (High-Level)

- Unit Trust
  - Potential pass-through: trust distributes income to unitholders; CGT discount may apply at unitholder level (facts dependent).
  - Streaming/distribution flexibility per deed; watch fixed vs discretionary trust rules.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Company tax rate applies; dividends to shareholders (franking credits may apply).
  - CGT at company level; no individual-level CGT discount inside company; shareholder-level outcomes on share disposal vary.

### QLD Duties (Transfer & Landholder Duty)

- Unit Trust
  - Property acquisition: transfer duty payable.
  - Changes in significant interests in the trust (unit transfers) may trigger landholder duty depending on thresholds and asset values.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Property acquisition: transfer duty payable.
  - Share transfers can trigger landholder duty where significant interests in a landholding entity change.

### QLD Land Tax

- Unit Trust
  - Land tax assessed; rates/thresholds and surcharges may differ for trusts; trustee liable with rights of indemnity from trust assets.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Land tax assessed at company rates/thresholds; no trust-specific surcharges, but company rates apply.

### GST (AU Context)

- Unit Trust
  - Residential rent input-taxed; new residential sales/development commonly subject to GST (margin scheme may be available); commercial leasing generally taxable if registered.
  - Trustee registers for GST where required.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Same GST profile as above; company registers for GST where required.

### Finance & Lender Preferences

- Unit Trust
  - Common in AU property; lenders may require director guarantees and tighter LVRs; trustee resolutions/borrowing powers must be clear.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Familiar to lenders/insurers; director guarantees common; may achieve similar or slightly more straightforward bank processes.

### Distributions & Cash Flow

- Unit Trust
  - Distributions per deed; can stream classes of income (subject to rules); flexible timing.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Dividends declared by board; franking credit management relevant; more rigid dividend rules.

### Entry/Exit Mechanics

- Unit Trust
  - Unit issuance/redemption or transfer; trust deed controls; QLD landholder duty considerations on significant interest changes.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Share issuance/redemption (limited) or transfer; constitution/shareholders' agreement control; QLD landholder duty on significant interest changes.

### Administration & Setup

- Unit Trust
  - Requires trust deed; corporate trustee setup; bank/trading accounts in trustee name as trustee for the trust.
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Company incorporation; constitution; bank/trading accounts in company name.

### Use Cases

- Unit Trust
  - Investor-friendly distributions; pass-through economics; flexible capital accounts; property syndications (often wholesale).
- `Pty Ltd`
  - Operating company or evergreen vehicle; straightforward governance; institutional lender comfort; simpler financial reporting.

## SPV Strategy

- SPV-per-deal: Ring-fences risk; simplifies exit and financing per asset; admin per deal.
- Evergreen vehicle: Operational continuity; capital recycling; manage legacy assets and valuation/exits carefully.

## Decision Factors (QLD Callouts)

- Duty/Landholder: Consider implications of unit/share transfers vs direct property transfers.
- Land Tax: Trust vs company rate/surcharge differences.
- GST: Development and commercial leasing registration; margin scheme eligibility.
- Lender Terms: Borrowing powers in deed/constitution; guarantees; LVR/DSCR covenants.
- Exit Path: Unit/share sale vs asset sale; tax/duty consequences.
