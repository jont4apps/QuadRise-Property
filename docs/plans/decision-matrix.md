# Decision Matrix — Partnership Vehicle

> Template for setting decision rights and thresholds. Adapt to chosen structure (Unit Trust with corporate trustee or Pty Ltd company). General information only — obtain professional advice.

## Decision Classes

- Ordinary Decisions
  - Examples: Operating budget within limits; vendor selection; contracts under delegated authority; minor capex; property management decisions.
  - Proposed Threshold: 51% of capital (or majority of partners).
  - Quorum: >66% of capital present.
  - Delegations: Manager/Treasurer up to specified limits.
- Major Decisions
  - Examples: Acquisitions/disposals; new debt/refinance; distributions outside policy; capex above threshold; hedging.
  - Proposed Threshold: 75% of capital.
  - Quorum: >75% of capital present.
  - Additional: Written resolutions permitted.
- Reserved Matters
  - Examples: Admit/exit partners; amend constitutive documents; change strategy/mandate; related-party transactions beyond caps; dissolution.
  - Proposed Threshold: 90–100% of capital.
  - Quorum: >80–90% of capital present.
  - Conflict Management: Disinterested approvals for related-party items.

## Information & Reporting Rights

- Monthly ops dashboard: NOI, DSCR, occupancy, arrears, covenant headroom.
- Quarterly financials + investor letter; annual plan and optional audit.
- Access to books/records with reasonable notice.

## Cash Management & Reserves

- Waterfall: Gross receipts → Opex → Debt service → Reserves (opex/capex/interest/tax) → Preferred return (if any) → Profit splits.
- Reserves policy: Target months of opex/interest; capex program; tax set-asides.
- Capital calls: Notice period; cure window; consequences (dilution/penalty/loans).

## Conflicts & Related Parties

- Disclosure before engagement; benchmarking of terms; caps on fees.
- Disinterested vote threshold (e.g., 75%) for approvals.

## Notes

- Voting basis: Per capita vs by capital — choose one and stick with it.
- Quorum and thresholds: Tune for partner count and risk appetite.
- Alignment: Ensure lender covenants and constitutive documents are consistent.
