## 4. Red Flag: Title Under Peter Dixon

This is the most urgent issue to resolve.

**What you were told:** Title will be under Peter Dixon.  
**The problem:** If you pay $300K for land but title is in Peter's name alone, you have **no legal ownership** of that asset. You've made a gift.

**What it should be:**

- A new SPV (company or unit trust) that all three partners are members/unitholders of, OR
- Tenants-in-common on title (all three names), with percentage ownership clearly defined

**Most likely explanation:** This may be a shorthand for "Peter's company/trust will hold the asset" rather than Peter personally. But you must verify this explicitly and have it confirmed in writing.
